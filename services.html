The counter

Pork is what we do. Beef, lamb, poultry, sausages, pies and a hog roast in the back yard are how we round it out.

i.

Local pork — on the bone & off

The shop is built on pork. Loin chops, joints, belly, shoulder, ribs, trim. Cut from local Norfolk pigs, hung and prepared in the back of the shop. Tell us what you're cooking — a Sunday roast, a slow braise, a barbecue — and we'll set you up with the right cut at the right weight.

Counter · cut to order

ii.

House sausages

Mixed and cased on the premises in small batches. The mince and the seasoning go in the back room in the morning, the cases get filled at the linker, and what's made by lunchtime is what's on the counter that afternoon. Plain pork, traditional, and seasonal specials when the mood takes us.

Made on site · made fresh

iii.

Pies, sausage rolls & savouries

Hot pork pies, hot sausage rolls, cold pies for the cabinet. The pastry is rolled in the back, the meat is from the counter, and they go in the oven through the day so what comes out is what people take home. Best between mid-morning and mid-afternoon — call ahead if you want one warm at lunchtime.

Hot & cold · made on site

iv.

Hog roasts to order

The hog-roast service runs alongside the shop. A whole pig is slow-cooked overnight on the spit and arrives at your event ready to carve. Weddings, anniversaries, christenings, big birthdays, village hall do's, works parties — anything Norfolk and the surrounding area.

We size the pig to the headcount, talk through the apples-sauce-and-stuffing question, and quote you on the call. Book in advance: weekends in summer fill up fast.

Bookings · 01553 773 536

v.

Beef, lamb & poultry

The counter isn't only pork. Norfolk beef hung on the bone, lamb in season, chicken and the occasional turkey. Steaks cut to whatever thickness suits the pan. Joints butterflied or rolled to spec. Special orders welcome — ring us and we'll have it ready.

Special orders welcome

vi.

Cheese & cooked meats

A small, edited range of cheeses and house-cooked meats — boiled hams, gammon, tongue, savoury bits — for the customer who wants the rest of the picnic, the cold-board, or the working lunch from the same shop.

Cabinet

vii.

Special orders & advance bookings

Match-day breakfasts, batches for a family gathering, a particular cut for a recipe, a freezer pack to stock up — give us a couple of days' notice and it'll be wrapped and ready for the morning you want it. The shop's number is the fastest route.

Call ahead · 01553 773 536

Ordering the easy way

Pop in. Or pick up the phone.

The counter handles most of it. For hog roasts, batches, or anything that needs prep, give us a ring a day or two ahead and we'll get it sorted. We don't ship and we don't deliver — but most of King's Lynn is walking distance from Norfolk Street.
