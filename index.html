A note from the shop

A Norfolk-Street butcher, not a brand.

The shop sits at number 108, half-way along Norfolk Street, two doors down from the corner. No marketing department, no online order portal, no minimum spend. The bell goes when you push the door. There's a man in an apron behind the counter, a slate with the day's specials chalked on it, and the smell of warm pastry from whatever's just come out of the oven.

If you want a pork chop the thickness of your thumb, ask. If you want a leg of lamb butterflied for the barbecue, ask. If you want fifty sausages for Saturday's match-day breakfast, give us a couple of days' notice and they'll be ready when you walk in.

That's how Norfolk Street has always done it, and how we've always done it here.
