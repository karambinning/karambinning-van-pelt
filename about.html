About the shop

About Van Pelt

A Norfolk Street pork butcher, in King's Lynn, with the door open most days and the same approach to butchery the family has always had.

The Van Pelt name has been on Norfolk Street in King's Lynn for as long as most people on this side of town can remember. The shop sits at number 108, just down from the junction — a small front, a window full of what's on the counter that morning, and a bell on the door that rings when you push it open.

Inside, the shop is what an independent butcher should be: a glass cabinet, a slate with the day's price, scales on the counter, a rail of hooks behind, and a back room where the sausages get mixed and the pies go into the oven. Nothing has been theatrically restored. Nothing has been redesigned for a magazine. It works because it has always worked.

What we do, in plain terms

We're pork butchers first. Local Norfolk pork comes in on the bone, gets cut to whatever you ask for — chops, joints, belly, shoulder, trim. The sausages are mixed in the back of the shop in small batches and cased the same morning. The pies are made on the premises and kept hot under the counter until they go.

Beyond the pork, we keep beef, lamb and poultry on the counter, hung properly and cut to specification. We carry a small range of cheeses and cooked meats — hams, tongue, gammon — for the customer who wants the rest of the order from one shop.

Hog roasts

The hog-roast side of the business has been running for decades. A whole pig goes on the spit the night before, gets cooked low and slow, and arrives at your wedding, christening, summer party, village hall or works do ready to carve. We talk every booking through individually — the size of the pig is a function of how many you're feeding, and we'd rather you finished the lot than ended up with leftovers nobody wants.

If you're thinking about a hog roast, the easiest thing is to ring the shop. We'll talk you through the options and quote you on the spot.

What you won't find here

There is no online ordering. There is no app. We don't ship. We don't have a loyalty card. The shop is open when it's open, and you come in, and we sort you out.

That's the trade.
